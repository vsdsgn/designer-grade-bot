_Last updated: April 2025_

- Your Telegram username and user ID
- Your language setting
- Your answers to the design test
- Your payment status (if applicable)

### Why we collect it:
- To personalize your experience and grade results
- To generate your final report
- To keep a record of your progress
- To enable secure access and payment

### What we **don’t** do:
- We don’t share your data with third parties
- We don’t send you ads
- We don’t collect unnecessary personal data
